# Straits Dental Group — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Straits Dental Group is a Singapore-based dental practice operating three clinics in Orchard, Raffles Place, and Tai Seng. The group provides general, aesthetic, restorative, and children's dentistry alongside facial aesthetic treatments.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the practice's range of services more clearly, simplifying treatment discovery, and making clinic contact and appointment enquiries more accessible through a structured, patient-centred experience.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Homepage Messaging

**Previous Issue:** The original hero headline, "Patient-Centred Comprehensive Approach," communicated the practice's philosophy but did not immediately explain its range of dental services or provide a compelling starting point for visitors.

**Implemented Fix:** Introduced a clearer dental-care proposition with supporting information and a prominent appointment action. The updated hero helps visitors understand the practice's offering and identify their next step immediately.

### 2. Improved Service Organisation

**Previous Issue:** Although the original homepage presented multiple treatment categories, the service options were not sufficiently distinct or easy to compare at a glance.

**Implemented Fix:** Organised the four main dental service categories into a clear 2 × 2 grid, featuring general, aesthetic, restorative, and children's dentistry. Each category includes relevant treatment examples, helping patients identify suitable services without navigating through lengthy descriptions.

### 3. More Accessible Clinic Contact Options

**Previous Issue:** Clinic locations and contact options lacked prominence within the homepage journey, requiring visitors to navigate further before identifying a suitable branch or communication method.

**Implemented Fix:** Brought all three clinic locations forward, presenting their respective phone and WhatsApp contact details together. Added two persistent mobile contact actions to provide easier access to enquiries and appointment assistance.

## Design Improvements

The homepage redesign introduces several targeted improvements:

- **Stronger Visual Hierarchy:** Established a clearer progression from the main dental-care message to services, locations, and contact options.
- **Simplified Service Discovery:** Replaced less distinct treatment presentation with a structured, scannable four-category grid.
- **Improved User Experience:** Reduced the effort required to understand available treatments and identify the appropriate clinic.
- **Mobile-Friendly Contact Access:** Introduced persistent contact actions for convenient communication on smaller screens.
- **Consistent Content Organisation:** Created a more cohesive presentation of the practice's services, clinic information, and appointment pathways.

## Summary

The redesigned Straits Dental Group homepage addresses three primary communication and usability challenges: generic homepage messaging, insufficiently distinct service presentation, and limited prominence of clinic contact options.

These improvements establish a clearer, more organised patient journey while maintaining the practice's patient-centred positioning and making essential information easier to access.
